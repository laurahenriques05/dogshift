# Walking basics: a happier walk, together

Academy module ID: `walking-basics`.
Content owner: Carol. Reading time: approximately 2 minutes.

Learning objective: prepare a comfortable walk, allow exploration and recognize
that GPS statistics are estimates rather than an exercise prescription.

This first-release guide is archived from the Academy path to keep the Academy
focused on dog-training challenges. The old `/walks/learn` URL now returns to
Walks. Sections: before you step outside; let your dog explore; know when to
head home; one quiet sniffing break to try.

Knowledge check: Does missing GPS mean your dog has stopped moving?
Correct answer: No. Browser suspension or poor signal can interrupt positions.
Feedback: Check the walk yourself; missing positions cannot prove stationarity.

Historical completion rows remain in the database so existing family XP is
preserved; the archived guide no longer awards new Academy XP.
